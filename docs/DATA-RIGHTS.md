# Player data-rights request
Access rights vary by jurisdiction, identity-verification rules and corporate policy. Ask the relevant data controller through its official portal rather than emailing credentials or identity documents to researchers.

## Reusable request
> I request access to the personal information associated with my gaming account(s), including gameplay and interaction data, device and platform identifiers, any profile inferences, segmentation, scoring or automated decisions associated with my account, retention periods, processing purposes and categories of third-party recipients. Please also describe relevant choices or opt-out rights.

Do not paste responses containing personal information into public GitHub issues. If sharing with the atlas, redact all identifying details first and consider contributing only a description of categories that were or were not disclosed.

## Starting points
- Microsoft: https://account.microsoft.com/privacy
- Take-Two: https://www.take2games.com/data-request/
- Other publishers: follow the data-rights contact in their current published privacy policy.
